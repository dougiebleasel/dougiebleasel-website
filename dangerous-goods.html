Overview

Manage the risk, not just the paperwork.

This area brings the key requirements, guidance, records and digital tools for hazardous substances and dangerous goods together in one place.

Identify

Determine what hazardous substances or dangerous goods are present, where they are used and how people may be exposed.

Assess

Consider quantity, storage, handling, compatibility, exposure, ignition sources and other relevant risk factors.

Control

Apply suitable elimination, substitution, engineering, administrative and PPE controls and review whether the residual risk is acceptable.

IMS Requirements

What the system requires

These will eventually be driven directly from the controlled IMS documentation and linked to the source requirement.

Register hazardous materials

Hazardous substances and dangerous goods should be identified and maintained within an appropriate register.

Maintain current SDS

Current Safety Data Sheets should be readily accessible to workers who use, handle or may be exposed to the material.

Assess risk

Risk assessment should consider the material, task, environment, storage arrangements, exposure and emergency response needs.
