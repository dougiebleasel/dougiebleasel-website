Implementation Discovery

Now let's make it yours.

We already know what your business does and what management system you need. Now we need a little more detail about how your organisation actually works so Tiwili can build the system around you.

Setting up the management system for your organisation .
01 · Organisation

Tell us how you're structured.

These details allow Tiwili to use the correct company name, scope and organisational context throughout your system.

02 · Your Language

What do you call your people?

A management system shouldn't sound like it belongs to somebody else. Tell Tiwili the terminology your organisation actually uses.

03 · Responsibilities

Who makes the decisions?

Use role titles rather than people's names where possible. Tiwili will use these roles consistently throughout procedures, workflows and approval requirements.

04 · What You Already Have

We don't need to reinvent everything.

Tell us what already exists. Later, Tiwili will be able to analyse existing documents and data and decide what should be retained, improved or replaced.

05 · Technology

Where does your business already work?

This becomes especially important for Tiwili Embedded. We want the management system to fit your technology rather than forcing your people into another disconnected platform.

06 · Build Approach

How should we treat what already exists?

This tells the generation engine whether it should start fresh or preserve more of the organisation's existing structure and language.
