Setup 03 · Your Requirements

What does your management system need to satisfy?

Tell Certicium which jurisdictions, standards, contractual commitments and regulatory obligations apply to your organisation. We will use confirmed requirements as controlled context when building your IMS.

01 · Jurisdictions

Where do requirements apply?

Confirm the jurisdictions Certicium may use when determining applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Include countries and, where relevant, states, territories, provinces or other regulatory jurisdictions.
Controlled context: Certicium should not introduce an unconfirmed jurisdiction into generated management system content.
02 · Standards & Certification

Which standards matter to you?

Select standards your organisation is certified against, working towards, contractually required to meet, or wants Certicium to incorporate into the management system.

03 · Customer & Contract Requirements

What have you committed to customers?

Contracts and customer requirements can create obligations beyond legislation and certification standards.

Describe known obligations or requirements that Certicium should consider.
04 · Regulatory & Other Obligations

Are there other formal obligations?

Capture licences, permits, accreditations, regulator approvals and other obligations that may need to be controlled through the IMS.

05 · Source Material

What should Certicium rely on?

Identify the source material that supports your requirements. Proper document upload and evidence storage will be added when customer accounts and file storage are connected.

Requirement source documents

For the prototype, list the documents you expect to provide. Later these will be uploaded and linked directly to your requirements register.

Future Certicium behaviour: uploaded evidence can be analysed to suggest requirements, but suggested obligations should remain unconfirmed until reviewed by the customer.
