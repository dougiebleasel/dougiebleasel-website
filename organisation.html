03 · Governance

Who is responsible?

These responsibilities will be used throughout policies, procedures, workflows and approvals. Use role or group names, rather than individual people's names.

Management system owner

Overall responsibility for maintaining and improving the IMS.

Policy approver

Role or group authorised to approve management system policies.

Risk Management owner

Role or function responsible for the Risk Management framework.

Significant risk authority

Role or group authorised to accept or escalate Significant risks.

Incident Management owner

Role or function responsible for incident governance and investigation.

Corrective action oversight

Role or function responsible for monitoring actions through to closure.
